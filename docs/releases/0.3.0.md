## New Feature(s)
- Show the maximum of each metric over the whole recording on the history screen.

## Change(s)
- Drag on a history chart to zoom in to the selected time range; hold Shift and drag to move left or right.
- Synchronize the chart cursor across all GPUs.
- Choose a host on the recordings screen first, then manage its recordings.
- Show tabs only for the hosts being monitored, and make tabs easier to recognize.
- Show the time range between the zoom buttons on the monitor screen; the buttons step through fixed time ranges.
- Make it easier to select recordings and to pin processes.
- Keep the height of a curve unchanged when the chart is scrolled or zoomed.
- Show overlapping curves in alternating colors.
